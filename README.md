# noc-mobifone-automation-ticket
Well I'm lazy so i made this because typing out incident reports manually isn't badass
Just dump your alarm log here in here transpro "mobifone internal tool", and it will vomit out a formatted SmartW ticket for you. 

No setup, no thinking. Copy, paste, get back to doing nothing

**How to use:**
1. Export your alarm log from Transpro (.txt or .csv).
2. Upload it to the tool. 
3. Type the ticket number and root cause.
4. Click Copy -> Paste straight into SmartW.
5. Once fixed, hit "Close Ticket" to auto-generate the end report.
